Two AI-control evals, fenced in by ground truth

Built on a deterministic tile-simulation engine: classical logic runs in a fixed order, so two independent runs produce a bit-identical trace. That turns a control eval into something you can regress like a unit test — a real exfiltration is or isn't detected at an exact tick, a benign baseline does or doesn't false-alarm, a scheduler's safety claim is checked against the same leak oracle and the same measured costs every time. Both evals below are watchable — scrub them live.

Throughput ↔ safety scheduler

What this is not: a side channel on real silicon. It's a deterministic toy — a leaky-integrator cooling model, a synthetic carrier, a phase-folding detector, a small cluster. The contribution is the eval scaffolding and its determinism, not a novel attack. What's real: both evals are reproducible and checked against ground truth (a replayable oracle / a live instruction-set differential on the job costs).
